Maintenance & Safety
09 · Reliable Operation

Maintenance designed around uptime and traceability.

A reliable elevator is the result of planned inspections, competent intervention and clear reporting—not emergency repair alone.

Preventive Maintenance

  • Scheduled inspection and servicing
  • Door, drive and safety checks
  • Cleaning, adjustment and lubrication
  • Condition observations and recommendations

Responsive Support

  • Emergency call handling
  • Fault diagnosis and corrective repair
  • Spare-parts coordination
  • Service records and client communication
Safety requirements, inspection frequency and final scope are defined according to equipment condition, applicable requirements and the agreed maintenance contract.
